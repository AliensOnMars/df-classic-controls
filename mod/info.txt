[STEAM_TITLE:Classic Controls - 0.47-style keyboard play (DFHack)]
[STEAM_DESCRIPTION:Brings back the keyboard-driven play of Dwarf Fortress 0.47. The old keys work again (d designate, b build, p stockpiles, i zones, q k v t cursors, j u n o lists, with the 0.47 letters inside each menu), the new screens can be worked without the mouse (- and = move through lists, Enter chooses, Esc backs out, buttons are on letters), and a key list is shown on screen for whatever is open, so nothing has to be memorised. Covers designations, building, workshops and their tasks, stockpiles and their settings and links, zones and locations, hauling routes and minecarts, the military, work orders, nobles, trade, the info window, creature sheets and more. Where the new game lost an old screen it adds one (combat reports, classic per-dwarf labors). Needs DFHack (free on the Steam store): start the game through DFHack. The mod does not have to be added to a world: once installed it works in every fort, old or new. The mouse keeps working throughout. Built and tested on DF 53.17 with DFHack 53.17-r1, premium graphics.]
