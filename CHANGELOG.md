# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-19)

First release, for Dwarf Fortress 53.17 with DFHack 53.17-r1.

- The 0.47.05 top-level keys on the map, with a `?` main menu listing them.
- Designations (`d`): the old menu and letters, with priorities on `-` `=`,
  new designations replacing old ones, and the box marked on the map.
- Building (`b`): the old build menu and letters, a footprint and area
  preview at the keyboard cursor, keyboard material choice.
- Workshops: tasks on their old hotkeys, the add-task list remembering the
  last task, suspend, repeat, remove.
- Stockpiles (`p`): the old types, container limits, links, the full settings
  screen on keys, `x` to remove.
- Zones (`i`): every type, assignment, locations and their details, the name
  builder; zone keys whether the zone was opened with `q` or the mouse.
- Hauling (`h`): routes, stops placed at the cursor, minecart assignment,
  departure conditions, stockpile links and the items a stop loads.
- Military: squads, positions, orders (move, kill, patrol, burrows,
  training), schedules and routines, equipment and uniforms.
- The info window: every tab on keys, with the map's letters switching tabs;
  work orders (the old manager) with conditions and details, nobles, tasks,
  places, labor (work details, standing orders, chores, kitchen, stone),
  objects, justice.
- Creature sheets: the old `v` keys for every page.
- Trade and moving goods to the depot.
- Announcements and a combat reports screen (`r`) with scrolling.
- Classic labors: per-dwarf labors with skill-based defaults
  (`classic-labors` command, or `c` on the work details page).
- `k` names the material of the tile under the cursor; anything other than
  the bare map pauses the game.
